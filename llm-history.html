A Seminar/Reading Plan on the History of Language Modeling

Course Overview

While a number of organizations and researchers have been happy to adopt Large Language Models as tools for various purposes, many use cases remain largely ill advised. Many of the issue that are prevalent across industries and domains arise from a lack of basic knowledge surounding how these models produce outputs, and thus what their intended, best use cases might be.

This reading list covers the history of language modeling, from early collocational frequencies to contemporary large languag models. The objective is to cultivate students' intuitions behind the core tasks that these models are trained to solve, and thus train readers to look for poor (and good) applications of such technologies.

Objectives
  • Students will identify the through-line between different language modeling techniques as they have been proposed over the years."
  • Students will be able to demystify discussions of language modeling in order to make more informed decisions about when they feel comfortable deploying it as a technology.
