Critical AI Arguments Reader

Course Overview

Since the release of ChatGPT in 2022, public interest in the field of "Artificial intelligence" has steadily increased over time. A number of companies -- including OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, and others -- have capitalized on this interest to procure additional sources of funding and public support.

In a number of cases, these public initiatives (and marketing campaigns) by major, "frontier" AI "labs" have been couched in hyperbolic language. The current reading list is designed as an information innoculation against some of the claims made by these companies.

The reading level for this list is approximately appropriate for either a young graduate student, or else an advanced undergraduate student with background in some statistical methods (specifically regression based methods, of which deeplearning is a subset), and some knowledge of social science concepts.

Learning Objectives
  • Students will be able to identify critical arguments against AI related "hype"
  • Students will be able to articulate the limitations on known criticisms of AI algorithms
